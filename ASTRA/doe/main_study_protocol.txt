# MAIN STUDY - BATCH PROTOCOL

## Overview

**Study:** Explosives Recovery Optimization - Main Study (Multi-Level Pressure Dose-Response)
**Design:** Surface_type (steel/abs) x Pressure_g (5 levels: 10/50/100/200/300g), Solvent fixed = present (wet swab only, 100uL EtOH throughout -- dry swabs deliberately excluded per the pilot's own recommendation)
**Total new samples:** 44 (+ 10 negative controls = 54 total runs), organized into 5 batches
**Physical surfaces:** 8 REUSED from the pilot (Steel_01-04, ABS_01-04 -- already carry 50g/200g wet data, re-cleaned before reuse) + 4 NEW (Steel_05-06, ABS_05-06)
**Pooling note:** the pilot's own existing 50g/200g wet-only samples (16 rows: 4 steel + 4 ABS at each level) are pooled in at analysis time (`main_study_analysis.R`, `Study` covariate) -- they are NOT re-collected here. This is why the reused surfaces are only tested at the 3 NEW levels (10/100/300g) in this study, while the 4 brand-new surfaces are tested at all 5 levels.
**Design source:** `doe_nested_design.R` (generator) -> `main_study_design_nested.csv` (master lab-notes file, live working document) / `main_study_batch_checklist.csv` (this document's own source data)
**Status as of this draft:** Batches 1-2 COMPLETE (12-13 Aug 2026, recorded below as a historical record); Batch 3 collected but not yet swabbed/dated in the design file; Batches 4-5 not yet started.

## Pre-Experiment Setup (15 minutes)

### Pressure Calibration -- 5 LEVELS this time (vs. 2 in the pilot)

Practice applying and holding each of the following on the balance before starting. Contact area is unchanged from the pilot (same Q-tip / ASTRA fixture), so pressure (kPa) scales linearly with mass:

| Target mass | Force (N) | Approx. pressure (kPa) | Literature context |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10g +/- 2g  | ~0.10 N | ~1.3 kPa  | VeryLow -- well below the pilot's own "sub-optimal" 50g level; tests the bottom of the dose-response curve |
| 50g +/- 5g  | ~0.49 N | ~6.7 kPa  | Low -- the pilot's own original "low" level (reused-surface pooling point) |
| 100g +/- 10g | ~0.98 N | ~13.4 kPa | Medium -- new intermediate level, midway between the pilot's two original levels |
| 200g +/- 10g | ~1.96 N | ~26.8 kPa | High -- the pilot's own original "high" level (reused-surface pooling point) |
| 300g +/- 15g | ~2.94 N | ~40.2 kPa | VeryHigh -- extends beyond the pilot's own range, tests whether recovery continues to rise, plateaus, or falls |

- [ ] Practice all 5 weights until consistent; mark/memorize swab angle and hand position for each
- [ ] Confirm balance is levelled and zeroed

- [ ] Calculate: 50uL x 200 ng/uL = 10,000 ng per surface (unchanged from the pilot)
- [ ] Verify standard is well-mixed, fresh aliquot per batch

### Environmental Conditions

- [ ] Verify stable conditions (no drafts, consistent temp)

## Batch Workflow Overview

**IMPORTANT:** `main_study_design_nested.csv` is already sorted in the REAL execution order (negative control FIRST within each batch's surface-type group, then samples in randomized `TestSequence` order) -- unlike the pilot, NC rows are already built into this file, no manual insertion needed. Always check this file (or the printable `main_study_batch_checklist.csv`) for the current batch.

**Batches 1-3** (all 12 physical surfaces active -- 6 steel: 4 reused + 2 new; 6 ABS: 4 reused + 2 new):
1. Swab the NC host surface FIRST (while still clean from the previous cleaning cycle, before any spiking for this batch's steel group)
2. Spike all 6 steel surfaces (2 min)
3. Dry 5 minutes
4. Test all 6 steel surfaces **in the order shown in the CSV** (TestSequence)
5. Swab the ABS NC host surface
6. Spike all 6 ABS surfaces (2 min)
7. Dry 5 minutes
8. Test all 6 ABS surfaces **in the order shown in the CSV**
9. Clean all 12 surfaces (15-20 min)

**Batches 4-5** (only the 4 NEW surfaces active -- 2 steel: Steel_05/06; 2 ABS: ABS_05/06; testing their own 50g/200g levels, since the reused surfaces already have this data from the pilot):
Same sequence as above, scaled down to 2 surfaces per type.

**Tip:** Keep `main_study_design_nested.csv` (or the checklist) open on a tablet/laptop during execution.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Standard doesn't dry in 5 minutes**
- Solution: Extend drying time to 7-10 minutes; note in data sheet for all affected samples

**Problem: Pressure difficult to maintain at the new VeryLow (10g) or VeryHigh (300g) extremes**
- Solution: 10g is extremely light (barely more than the swab's own weight) -- practice extensively before Batch 1; 300g is firm but should still be comfortably held with a normal grip. Re-practice on the balance and adjust hand position/angle as needed.

**Problem: Can't remember which surface to test next, or which surfaces are active this batch**
- Solution: Keep `main_study_design_nested.csv` open on a tablet/laptop; remember Batches 1-3 use all 12 surfaces, Batches 4-5 use only the 4 new surfaces (Steel_05/06, ABS_05/06)

**Problem: Surface contamination between tests**
- Solution: Extend cleaning time, add extra rinse step; verify dryness before re-spiking

**Problem: EtOH evaporates from swab during test**
- Solution: Apply EtOH immediately before swabbing; work more quickly (but still consistently) -- Solvent is "present" for every single sample in this study, there is no dry-swab fallback condition

**Problem: A reused surface (Steel_01-04/ABS_01-04) shows visible wear from pilot use**
- Solution: Document the condition before use; the study's own reuse-trend check (`Diagnostics/reuse_trend_check_corrected.R`) found no significant degradation trend up to 6 physical uses, and this study extends that to ~7-8 uses -- proceed unless damage is severe enough to plausibly change swabbing contact area/quality, in which case flag and consider substituting.
